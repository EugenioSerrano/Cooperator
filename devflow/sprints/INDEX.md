# Sprints — Index

**Methodology version:** 5.2

Sprint records (`SPRINT-NNN-<description>.md`), one per sprint (§5.12):
planning records — no approval, no manifest — created only on a human's
request. **The next free `NNN` is claimed here** (§5.15): the next number
after the highest listed in any section below. A number once used — that of a
`closed` or `cancelled` sprint included — is never reused or renumbered; gaps
stay gaps.

Each sprint is listed under its `status`, and its row moves when the status
changes; the record's own `status` is the truth.
