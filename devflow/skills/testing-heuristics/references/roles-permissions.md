# Roles & Permissions

Applies when the story involves different actors or roles with different
levels of access.

## How to apply it

1. Take the roles and permissions explicit in the US or documented in
   `devflow/analysis/personas/`.
2. Generate one scenario per relevant role performing the story's main action,
   verifying that the result is the expected one for that role (allowed,
   denied, restricted).
3. Include attempts by a role without permission (negative) and, where
   applicable, escalation or impersonation cases when the story mentions them.
4. If roles and permissions are documented neither in the US nor in the
   analysis families, do not invent them — record an open question in the
   story analysis.
